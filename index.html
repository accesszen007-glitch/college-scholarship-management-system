FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ScholarEase?

ScholarEase is a college scholarship management platform that helps students discover scholarships, apply online and track applications.

Can students track their applications?

Yes. Students can view submitted, under-review, approved and rejected applications from their dashboard.

Can administrators manage scholarships?

Yes. Administrators can create scholarships, review applications, verify documents and generate reports.

Is ScholarEase mobile friendly?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
